MATERIALS / FIELD NOTE

Minimalism has room for character, warmth and imperfection.

A restrained interior need not feel cold. The natural variation in timber, woven material and plaster creates the depth that flat surfaces often lack.

The quieter
details.

A successful palette is built on relationships: warm and cool, smooth and textured, light and dark. Each element helps the others become more legible.

In the end, a well-composed room leaves space for the objects and rituals that make it personal.
