MATERIALS / FIELD NOTE

Materials quietly shape our relationship with place.

The feel of a surface, the depth of a grain and the weight of a fabric all contribute to a room's character.

details.

When textures are chosen with intention, they can define a threshold, lend intimacy to a large space or highlight a particular view.

Natural materials reward careful selection, and their variation can be a strength rather than something to conceal.
